How it reads

Three passes, then one answer you can check.

01

Watch

Sampled frames are checked for logos, objects and on-screen text. Each hit keeps its box, its confidence and its frame number.

02

Listen

Speech is transcribed and sound events are classified, so a brand named out loud is captured even when it never appears on screen.

03

Cross-check

The signals are fused into one reading. When they disagree, the interface says so instead of averaging the doubt away.

What you get

A reading with receipts.

No opaque percentage and no clean-looking guess. Each brand carries the signal that found it, the time it was seen and how strong that signal was.
